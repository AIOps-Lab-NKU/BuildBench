Registration rules

One captain, one team

  • One captain account manages the team.
  • Teams may contain 1–5 people, including the captain.
  • Every member email is required.
  • An email cannot appear in another team.
  • The leaderboard displays the team name only.
Roster lock

The organizer will announce when rosters become immutable. Until then, the captain may update non-captain members.
