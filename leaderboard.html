Scoring

The evaluation contains 74 easy, 111 medium and 15 hard cases. Weighted score = easy passes × 1 + medium passes × 3 + hard passes × 5. Pass rate = cases passed / 200.

Time and tokens

Time is summed across each team's final effective case runs; it excludes queueing and is not elapsed calendar time. Tokens are recorded model usage from those runs. Cached input is included in input, not added again. M = one million.

Institutions

Institutions follow team registration records. All participating institutions are listed for joint teams.
